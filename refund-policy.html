Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated 08/28/2026. Applies to purchases made on or after 09/01/2026.

The short version. Cancel any time in two clicks from your account page. First subscription payment refundable within 14 days, first annual payment within 30 days. The custom itinerary service is refundable in full before research starts, and partially after that.

This policy is a starting template and not legal advice. Have it reviewed by a licensed attorney before launch. It forms part of our Terms of Service.

1. How to cancel a subscription

Sign in, open your account page and select "Cancel subscription". It takes two clicks. There is no cancellation form, no retention call and no requirement to email us first.

Cancelling stops the next renewal. Your Pro access continues to the end of the period you have already paid for. If you would rather cancel by email, write to hello@wildroottripstudio.com from the address on your account and we will process it within one business day.

3. Custom itinerary service refunds

This is a one-time service with real human research behind it, so the refund position changes as work progresses. We tell you which stage you are at in every email.

Custom itinerary refund stages
StageRefundWhy
Before we begin research100 percentNothing has been produced. Usually the first 2 business days after your brief.
During research, before first draft50 percentResearch hours have been spent but you have received nothing yet.
After the first draft is deliveredNo refund, but unlimited corrections of factual errorsThe deliverable is in your hands. Anything factually wrong we fix at no charge, without limit.
We miss the delivery date by more than 5 business days100 percent, or 50 percent if you keep the planYour choice. We will offer it without you asking.
Your trip is cancelled after deliveryNo refundThe work is done. We will re-date the plan for a future trip to the same destination at no charge, within 12 months.

What "factual error" means. A site we said opens at 9 AM that opens at 10 AM, a drive time that is materially wrong, a permit requirement we missed. We fix those free, any number of times. A change of preference, such as deciding you would rather go somewhere else, is a revision and is charged at the rate on the pricing page.

4. What is never charged for

  • Creating an account.
  • The free tier, at any point.
  • Reading any guide, destination page, sample itinerary or checklist.
  • Support for a billing problem, including one we caused.
  • Exporting your data when you leave.

5. What happens to your trips

Cancelling a subscription does not delete anything. You keep read access to every trip you created and full editing on the two most recently updated, which is the free tier allowance. During your final paid period you can export a PDF of every trip, and a full data export is available on request at any time.

Deleting your account is separate and permanent. We remove your content within 90 days, except for payment records we must keep for 7 years for US tax purposes. Tell us at privacy@wildroottripstudio.com if you want an export before deletion; we will send it before anything is removed.

6. How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method. We approve or decline within 3 business days of your request, and once approved the money typically appears in 5 to 10 business days depending on your bank. We do not issue store credit in place of a refund unless you ask for it.

Currency is USD. If your bank converted the original charge, the refunded amount in your local currency may differ slightly because exchange rates move. That difference is your bank's, not a deduction by us.

7. Chargebacks

Please contact us before filing a chargeback. Almost every dispute we see is a forgotten renewal or an unrecognized descriptor on a statement, and both are resolved by email in under a day. Filing a chargeback triggers a bank process that takes weeks and locks your account while it runs. We will always try the simple route first.

8. Trip suppliers are not us

This policy covers money you pay to Wildroot Trip Studio. It does not cover flights, hotels, rental cars, tours or insurance. Those are separate contracts with those companies, on their own cancellation terms, and we have no ability to refund them. See our Disclaimer.

9. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under applicable consumer protection law, including any statutory cancellation right in your state or country. Where the law gives you more than this policy does, the law wins.

10. How to request a refund

Email hello@wildroottripstudio.com from the address on your account with the charge date and amount, or use the contact form and select "Billing or subscription question". You can also call +1 (720) 555-0148, Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM Mountain Time.
