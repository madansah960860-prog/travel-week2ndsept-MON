The part of your job that should not take four hours

Agents we talk to spend most of their unbilled time doing the same three things: rebuilding a day plan in a word processor, recalculating drive times by hand, and reformatting the whole document when a client changes one night.

Reusable trip templates

Save any plan as a template. The Tuscany week you have sold eleven times becomes a starting point, not a blank page, and updating the template updates nothing already sent.

Client-ready output

Export a branded PDF with your logo, your colors and your contact details in the footer. Or send a live link that updates as you revise the plan.

Changes that do not cascade

Move a stop and the travel times, the day totals and the trip budget recalculate. No manual arithmetic and no version confusion in an email thread.

What the professional plans add

White-label client documents

Your logo, your brand colors, your agency name and contact block. Wildroot branding is removed entirely on the White Label tier, and no Wildroot marketing appears in anything your client receives.

Multi-client workspace

Every trip is filed under a client record with notes, dates and status. Filter by departure date to see what needs attention this week.

Team seats

Add colleagues with their own logins. Trips can be assigned, shared internally, and handed over cleanly when someone is on leave.

Supplier notes

Attach a private note to any stop that never appears in the client-facing document: confirmation numbers, supplier contacts, the reason you picked that hotel.

Bulk PDF delivery

Export every trip departing in a given window at once, which matters in the week before a group departure.

Your commercial relationships stay yours

We do not insert affiliate links into your client documents, we do not contact your clients, and we do not use your itineraries to build our own public library. Your client list is yours.

What is not included

Wildroot is planning software. It is not a booking engine, a GDS, a CRM, an accounting system or a payment processor. We do not hold client funds, issue tickets or act as a seller of travel. If you need those, keep using the systems you have; Wildroot sits alongside them.

Getting started

  1. A 30-minute demo call with one of us, not a sales team.
  2. A 14-day trial workspace with your branding already applied.
  3. We import up to three of your existing itineraries as templates at no charge.
  4. You decide. If it is not right, delete the workspace and nothing is charged.
