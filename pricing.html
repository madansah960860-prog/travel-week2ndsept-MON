What happens if you cancel Pro

Your trips are not deleted. You keep read access to all of them and full editing on the two most recently updated, which is the Free tier limit. Nothing is held hostage and you can export a PDF of every trip during your final billing period.

What we do not charge for

  • Reading any guide, destination page or sample itinerary
  • The packing checklists and free tools
  • Creating an account
  • Customer support for a billing problem

The custom itinerary service

Some trips are worth handing to a person. An anniversary, a first international trip with children, a two-week route with a lot of moving parts. That is what this is for.

How it works

  1. You send the brief

    Dates, departure airport, budget, party, mobility, and what you care about. A short form, about ten minutes.

  2. We research and write it

    Five to seven business days. Real research against current opening hours, permits and seasonal closures.

  3. One revision call

    Thirty minutes on a video call. We change what is not right and rewrite the affected days.

  4. You receive the final plan

    An editable Wildroot trip plus a print-ready PDF, and three months of Pro so you can keep changing it.

How we make money

Four sources, in order of size. This is the complete list.

  1. Pro subscriptions. The majority of our revenue.
  2. Affiliate commissions from hotel, flight, rental car and tour bookings made through links on our site. You pay the same price; the booking company pays us.
  3. The custom itinerary service. A one-time fee for human-researched planning.
  4. Travel agent and white-label plans. Seat-based subscriptions for professionals.

We do not run display advertising anywhere on this site. If that ever changes, ads will be clearly labeled, kept away from the planner, and never placed on legal pages.

We do not sell your personal information, we do not accept payment for editorial placement, and no partner reviews our guides before publication.

Common questions about billing

Is there a free trial of Pro? No, because the Free tier is the trial and it does not expire.

Can I switch between monthly and annual? Yes, at any renewal. Mid-term switches are prorated.

Do prices change? If we raise the price, existing subscribers keep their rate for at least twelve months and are told by email at least thirty days ahead.

How do I cancel? From your account page in two clicks. No email required, no retention call.
