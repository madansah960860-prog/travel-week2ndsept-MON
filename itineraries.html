What is inside every Wildroot itinerary

Time-stamped stops

A start time for each stop, chosen around opening hours, light and crowds rather than convenience.

Travel time between stops

Driving, walking or transit, with distance in miles, so the day adds up to something possible.

An honest daily cost

In USD, for a stated party size, with a clear list of what the number leaves out.

The part most guides skip

What to book ahead, what to skip if you are short on time, and where the plan usually breaks.

Six more plans, summarized

These load directly into the planner with their stops and timings already in place. The summaries below tell you the shape of each trip before you open it.

Lisbon Long Weekend, 4 days

  • Portugal
  • 2 travelers
  • $392 activities

Built for a Thursday-night departure from the US East Coast and a Monday-night return, using two PTO days. Day one is deliberately light because you land jet-lagged in the morning.

Shape: Alfama and the miradouros on foot, a full day in Belem and the Time Out Market, a Sintra day trip with a timed ticket, and a final morning in Principe Real before the airport.

Big Sur Coastal Drive, 3 days

  • California
  • 2 travelers
  • $268 activities

Ninety miles of Highway 1, driven north to south so the ocean is on your right and every pullout is a right turn. That single decision changes the trip more than any stop on it.

Shape: Carmel to Bixby Bridge, Point Lobos and Pfeiffer Beach timed for low tide, McWay Falls at golden hour, and an exit through Cambria.

Banff and the Icefields Parkway, 5 days

  • Alberta, Canada
  • 4 travelers
  • $1,140 activities

Two nights in Banff, one on the parkway, two in Jasper. Built around the Moraine Lake shuttle reservation system, which is the constraint everything else has to fit around.

Shape: Lake Louise at sunrise, Moraine Lake by shuttle, Peyto and Bow Lake, the Athabasca Glacier, and Maligne Canyon on the final morning.

New Orleans in Three Days

  • Louisiana
  • 2 travelers
  • $486 activities

A long weekend that spends most of its time outside the French Quarter, because that is where the city is most itself. Two dinners are reserved before you fly.

Shape: the Garden District on foot, a Whitney Plantation visit, Frenchmen Street in the evening, a swamp tour, and beignets timed for the quiet hour.

Dolomites Hiking Base, 6 days

  • Northern Italy
  • 2 travelers
  • $744 activities

One base in Val Gardena, six day hikes of increasing difficulty, and a rest day in the middle that most itineraries leave out. Trailhead parking fills before 8 AM, so every start time is early.

Shape: Seceda ridgeline, Alpe di Siusi, the Tre Cime loop, Lago di Braies at dawn, a rifugio lunch, and a cable car day for the rest day.

Mexico City for Eaters, 4 days

  • Mexico
  • 2 travelers
  • $418 activities

Structured around meals rather than sights, with museums placed between them. Day one is gentle because the city sits at 7,350 feet and altitude affects most visitors from sea level.

Shape: a Roma and Condesa market walk, the Museo Nacional de Antropologia, Coyoacan on a Saturday, a Teotihuacan half day, and one reserved tasting dinner.
