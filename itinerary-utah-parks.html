Three days in Moab, built around sunrise

Arches, Canyonlands and a half day on the Colorado River, with one base for all three nights. Built for four travelers in one vehicle between April and May or September and October.

  • 3 days
  • 4 travelers
  • 210 miles driving
  • $854 in activities and meals
Copy this into the planner Read the parks guide

Costs exclude flights, lodging and the rental vehicle.

Book before you fly

  • Arches timed entry, the day the window opens
  • Moab lodging, three months out for spring and fall
  • The rafting half day, three to four weeks out
  • An America the Beautiful annual pass, online or at the gate

Download before you drive

  • Offline maps for the entire Moab region
  • The official park app for both parks
  • Your itinerary as a PDF or on paper
  • Sunrise and sunset times for your exact dates

Pack for this plan

  • One gallon of water per person per day, in the car
  • A headlamp for the pre-dawn start on Day 1
  • Wide-brim hat, high-SPF sunscreen, sunglasses
  • Shoes with real grip; slickrock is slippery when dusty

The plan, day by day

Every day starts before the heat and pauses through the middle of it. That is not a stylistic choice; it is how the desert works.

Day 1

Arches, before the heat

62 miles · 5.5 miles hiking
  • 5:30 AM Leave Moab for the park Timed entry is not required before 7 AM in most seasons, which is one more reason to start early. Confirm the current window before you go. Drive 20 min · 11 miles
  • 6:00 AM Delicate Arch trail 3 miles round trip with 480 feet of climb across open slickrock. No shade whatsoever. Headlamps for the first mile, then sunrise on the arch itself. Drive 20 min · 11 miles
  • 9:30 AM Windows Section loop A flat one-mile loop past North Window, South Window and Turret Arch. Add the primitive trail on the back side for the best view and half the people. Drive 12 min · 6 miles
  • 11:00 AM Balanced Rock and Park Avenue Two short paved stops on the way out of the park. Ten to fifteen minutes each is plenty at this hour. Drive 25 min · 14 miles
  • 12:30 PM Lunch and reset in Moab Refill every water bottle, top off the gas tank, and take the hottest three hours of the day indoors. This pause is part of the plan. Drive 15 min · 9 miles
  • 6:15 PM Corona Arch at golden hour 3 miles round trip on BLM land outside the park, with two short cable and ladder sections. No entry fee and a fraction of the crowd.
Estimated day cost
$164 4 travelers · timed entry, fuel, 2 meals · excludes lodging
Day 2

Island in the Sky

88 miles · 8 miles hiking
  • 5:45 AM Drive to Canyonlands Highway 313 climbs 2,000 feet onto the mesa. Watch for deer on the shoulders in the half light. Drive 45 min · 32 miles
  • 6:30 AM Mesa Arch sunrise A half-mile loop to an arch that catches reflected light from the canyon below. Photographers line up 45 minutes early for the front row; arrive later and stand behind them. Drive 15 min · 8 miles
  • 8:30 AM Grand View Point trail Two miles round trip along the mesa rim with almost no elevation change. The single best view in the park and worth the full hour. Drive 20 min · 11 miles
  • 10:45 AM Upheaval Dome overlook One mile round trip with a steep rocky section to the first overlook. Geologists still disagree about whether it is a meteor crater or a collapsed salt dome. Drive 35 min · 22 miles
  • 12:45 PM Dead Horse Point State Park A separate state park with its own entry fee, not covered by the federal pass. The rim loop is 5 miles and mostly flat; the main overlook is a two-minute walk from parking. Drive 45 min · 32 miles
  • 4:00 PM Back to Moab, rest Shower, eat early, and nap. The last stop is late and worth being awake for. Drive 25 min · 15 miles
  • 9:00 PM Stargazing outside town Both parks are certified International Dark Sky Parks. Use a red-light headlamp and give your eyes twenty minutes to adjust.
Estimated day cost
$212 4 travelers · state park entry, fuel, 3 meals · excludes lodging
Day 3

River and rock art

60 miles · slower pace
  • 7:30 AM Check in at the rafting outfitter Most Moab operators run from a storefront in town and shuttle you to the put-in. Arrive 30 minutes before the listed departure. Shuttle 30 min · 18 miles
  • 8:30 AM Colorado River half day, Fisher Towers stretch Class II and III water below red rock spires. Family-friendly at normal flows. Wear quick-dry clothing and secure your sunglasses. Shuttle 30 min · 18 miles
  • 12:30 PM Lunch and dry off in Moab Some trips include a riverside lunch. Confirm when you book so you are not paying twice. Drive 15 min · 9 miles
  • 2:30 PM Potash Road petroglyph panels Roadside rock art created over thousands of years, visible from a pullout. Look and photograph. Never touch; skin oils accelerate the damage. Drive 25 min · 14 miles
  • 4:30 PM Fisher Towers trail, first mile The full trail is 4.4 miles round trip. Walking the first mile gets you underneath the towers without committing to the whole afternoon. Drive 40 min · 24 miles
  • 7:30 PM Last dinner in Moab Kitchens close earlier than most visitors expect, and waits run long in spring. Aim to be seated by 8:00 PM.
Estimated day cost
$478 4 travelers · rafting for four, fuel, 2 meals · excludes lodging

Where this plan usually breaks

The 5:30 AM start. Groups of four rarely leave on time. Set the alarm 30 minutes earlier than feels necessary.

Timed entry missed. If the Arches window has sold out, flip Days 1 and 2 and try the next-day release.

Summer heat. In July and August this plan is not safe as written. Cut Corona Arch and move Fisher Towers to sunrise.

If you have more days

Moab is one corner of a much larger loop. Each addition below is a genuine extra day, not a detour.

Day 4: Capitol Reef

Two and a half hours west. Far fewer visitors, a historic orchard you can pick fruit in, and the Scenic Drive as an easy afternoon.

Day 5 and 6: Bryce Canyon

The hoodoo amphitheater from the rim, then the Navajo Loop down into it. At 8,000 feet it is 20°F cooler than Moab, so pack accordingly.

Day 7 to 9: Zion

The Narrows, the Emerald Pools and Angels Landing if you win the permit lottery. Finish in Las Vegas for the flight home.
