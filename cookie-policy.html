Cookie Policy

Last updated 08/28/2026.

Please note. This policy is a starting template. It is not legal advice and should be reviewed by a licensed attorney, and re-audited against your live tag configuration, before launch.

Change your choice now. Open cookie preferences to review or change what you have allowed.

1. What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file a website asks your browser to store. It lets the site remember something between page loads, such as which cookie categories you have allowed. We also use local storage, which works similarly but stays on your device rather than being sent with every request.

2. Our approach

On your first visit we show a banner with three clear options: accept all, reject non-essential, and manage preferences by category. Until you choose, only strictly necessary items are set. Rejecting is exactly as easy as accepting, and both are one click.

We do not use cookie walls. Rejecting non-essential cookies gives you the full site, including the trip planner and every guide.

4. Third-party embeds

We load web fonts from Google Fonts and photography from image hosts. These requests reveal your IP address to those providers as a technical necessity of loading the file, but they do not set advertising cookies on this site. We do not embed social media widgets, chat widgets or video players that track you.

5. Changing or withdrawing consent

  • Use the cookie preferences link on this page or in the footer of any page.
  • Clear this site's cookies and local storage in your browser, which resets your choice and brings the banner back.
  • Set your browser to block cookies. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent sign-in and form submission from working.

We honor the Global Privacy Control signal. If your browser sends GPC, we treat it as an opt out of advertising for that browser, whatever the banner says.

6. Do Not Track

There is still no agreed industry standard for how sites should respond to a Do Not Track browser header, so we do not respond to it. We do respond to Global Privacy Control, which is the signal with legal recognition in California.

7. Changes to this policy

When we add or remove a cookie we update the tables above and the date at the top. Where a change expands what we collect, we will ask for consent again rather than assuming your previous choice covers it.
