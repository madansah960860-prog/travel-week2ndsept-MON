A small studio that thinks planning is the good part

Wildroot Trip Studio is an independent company in Boulder, Colorado. We build a trip planner, we write travel guides, and we tell you plainly how we get paid for both.

Why this exists

Wildroot started with a spreadsheet. In 2021 our founder was planning a two-week trip through the Scottish Highlands and kept running into the same problem: every planning tool was happy to hold a list of places, and none of them would tell her that the list was impossible.

Six stops looked reasonable in a column. Laid out against actual single-track roads it was nine and a half hours of driving, which meant the trip as written involved seeing almost nothing except a windshield. The spreadsheet that fixed it, with a travel-time column between every row, became the thing friends kept asking to borrow.

That is still the core idea. A plan is not a list of places you would like to see. A plan is a list of places plus the honest cost, in hours and dollars, of moving between them. Almost everything Wildroot does follows from taking that seriously.

What we believe about travel planning

Fewer things, done properly. Our most-followed itineraries have the fewest items per day. When we started measuring which plans people actually completed, this was the clearest pattern in the data and it surprised us.

Constraints first, destination second. Days off, budget and departure airport determine what is possible far more than enthusiasm does. Choosing the destination first is how people end up with a trip that does not fit.

An estimate labeled as an estimate is useful. An estimate presented as a fact is not. Every cost figure on this site is a planning range and is marked as one. Prices move, and pretending otherwise would make the numbers worse, not better.

Say who paid for what. If a link earns us money, we say so on the page you are reading, not only in the footer. If a recommendation would be different without the commission, it should not be a recommendation.

What we are not

We are not a travel agency, a tour operator or a seller of travel. We do not book flights, hotels, cars or tours, we do not hold client funds, and we do not issue tickets. If your booking has a problem, your contract is with the company you booked through and we cannot resolve it for you.

We are also not a review site. We do not rank hotels or restaurants, we do not accept payment for placement in our destination library, and no commercial partner reads our guides before publication.

How we make money, in plain English

Four sources. That is the entire list, in order of size.

  1. Pro subscriptions. $9 a month or $84 a year. This is most of our revenue and the reason the free tier can be a real product rather than a crippled demo.
  2. Affiliate commissions. Some links to hotels, flights, rental cars and tours are affiliate links. If you book through one, the booking company pays us a commission. You pay exactly the same price you would pay going direct.
  3. The custom itinerary service. A one-time fee, starting at $249, for a trip plan researched and written by a person here.
  4. Travel agent and white-label plans. Seat-based subscriptions for independent agents and small tour operators.

We do not run display advertising anywhere on this site. If we ever introduce it, ads will be clearly labeled, kept away from the planner, and never placed on legal pages. This page changes before that does.

Things we do not do: we do not sell your personal information, we do not accept money to rank a destination higher, and no hotel, airline or tour operator has editorial input into what we publish. If any of that ever changes, this page changes first.

Our editorial standards

These are the rules we hold ourselves to. If we break one, tell us at hello@wildroottripstudio.com and we will fix it publicly.

Bylines on everything

Every guide names its author and shows the publication date and the last update date. We do not republish old material with a new date.

Sources for anything checkable

Where a claim involves a permit, a fee, an opening time or a safety rule, we point you at the official source and tell you to verify it yourself.

Corrections stay visible

When we get something wrong we correct it in place and note what changed. We do not quietly edit and move on.

Disclosure at the point of use

Affiliate links are disclosed on the page that contains them, above the content, not buried in a footer link.

Real reviews only

Testimonials are collected after a trip has finished, from people who used the product. We publish the mixed ones. We do not write them ourselves.

No manufactured urgency

No countdown timers, no fake scarcity, no invented statistics, and no headline that promises something the page does not deliver.

Come and use the thing we built

The free tier is a real product. If it saves you an afternoon, we have done our job.
