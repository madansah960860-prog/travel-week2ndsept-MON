IMAGE AND LOGO NOTES (internal, not ad copy)
--------------------------------------------
Responsive display also needs images and a logo. Use warm natural photography
consistent with the site: forest, coast, mountain and open-road subjects in
soft daylight. Avoid cold blue grading, neon and heavy vignettes.

Required ratios:
  Landscape 1.91:1   - 1200 x 628 px minimum
  Square 1:1         - 1200 x 1200 px minimum
  Portrait 4:5       - 960 x 1200 px minimum, optional but recommended
  Logo square 1:1    - 1200 x 1200 px, transparent background
  Logo landscape 4:1 - 1200 x 300 px, transparent background

Do not place text over the supplied images. Google overlays the headline and
description itself, and burned-in text can cause a disapproval or crop badly.

COMPLIANCE CHECKLIST APPLIED TO THIS FILE
-----------------------------------------
- No all-caps words other than the acronyms US, USD, PDF and PTO.
- No exclamation points, no repeated punctuation, no emoji, no symbols.
- No reference to the ad itself and no use of "click here" or "click now".
- No superlatives and no unverifiable claims. No "best", "number one",
  "guaranteed" or similar.
- No fake urgency, fake scarcity, countdowns or invented statistics.
- No specific prices, fares, discounts or deals. Feature claims only.
- No airline, hotel, competitor or trademarked brand names.
- No phone numbers in any headline.
- Every line is written to stand alone, because Google mixes assets.
- Angles varied across the set: planning speed, day-by-day organization,
  budgeting, group and family trips, first-time travelers, road trips,
  and international travel departing from the US.
- Tone matches the site: calm, plain, unhurried, specific.
