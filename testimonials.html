Reviews, including the ones that sting

We ask for feedback after a trip has finished, not at signup, and we publish what comes back. The three and four star reviews are on this page too.

Read the reviews How we collect them

Reviews are edited only for length and typos, never for tone.

Asked after the trip

The request goes out two weeks after a traveler's return date, not at signup and not while they are excited about booking.

No incentives

We do not offer discounts, credits, entries or free months in exchange for a review. Nothing is given for writing one.

Nothing suppressed

Critical reviews are published alongside positive ones, with our reply where a reply is warranted.

Verified accounts

Every review is tied to a real account with a completed trip. We do not accept anonymous submissions.

What travelers said

Twelve reviews from the last two quarters, in the order they were received.

Our review policy in full

Reviews on this page come from Wildroot account holders who completed a trip planned with the product. The request is sent by email two weeks after the trip's end date, once, with no reminder.

What we edit

Length, obvious typos and anything that would identify a third party such as a named hotel employee. We do not edit tone, soften criticism or change the substance of what someone wrote.

What we never do

  • Offer discounts, credits, free months or prize entries in exchange for a review.
  • Write reviews ourselves, or ask staff, family or contractors to write them.
  • Remove a review because it is critical. Reviews are removed only for abuse, doxxing or a legal demand.
  • Display an aggregate star rating anywhere on the site. Twelve reviews is not a large enough sample to average honestly.

If you think a review here is not genuine

Email hello@wildroottripstudio.com with the reviewer's name and we will re-verify it against the account record and reply to you with what we find.

These commitments follow the FTC's guidance on endorsements and testimonials. Reviews that were incentivized in any way would be labeled as such; none on this page were.

Use the contact form and choose "Something else", or reply to the review request email we sent you.
